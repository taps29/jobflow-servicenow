# Sprint 3 – Business Logic & Client-Server Automation

## Objective

Enhance the JobFlow application by implementing business validation, reference relationships, and client-server automation using GlideAjax.

---

## Features

### Company Reference Refactoring

Converted the Company field into a reference field connected to the custom Company table to ensure relational data consistency.

---

### Recruiter Management

Implemented a Recruiter table containing recruiter information and linked it to Company records using reference fields.

---

### Duplicate Application Prevention

Developed a Before Insert Business Rule to prevent duplicate job applications for the same Company and Job ID combination.

---

### Auto Recruiter Population

Implemented a Client Script, Script Include and GlideAjax communication that automatically populates the Recruiter field when a Company is selected.

---

## Concepts Practiced

- Reference Fields
- Dictionary Entries
- Display Fields
- Business Rules
- GlideRecord
- GlideAjax
- Script Includes
- Client Scripts
- Client-Server Communication
- Data Validation

---

## Outcome

Sprint 3 transformed JobFlow from a basic CRUD application into a structured business application with automated validation and client-server interactions.
